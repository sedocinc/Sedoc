How to Remove GST or VAT from a Price

To remove GST or VAT from a tax-inclusive total, divide the total by (1 + rate ÷ 100). The result is the net price, and the difference is the tax. Switch the calculator to "Remove GST / VAT" to do this automatically.

Worked example (18% GST): a bill totals 11,800. Net price = 11,800 ÷ 1.18 = 10,000. GST = 11,800 − 10,000 = 1,800.

A common mistake: multiplying 11,800 by 18% gives 2,124, which is wrong, because 18% applies to the net price and not to the total that already includes tax. Always divide by 1.18.

GST vs VAT vs Sales Tax

GST (Goods and Services Tax), VAT (Value Added Tax) and sales tax are all consumption taxes added to the price of goods and services. The math is the same: only the name and the rate change by country. Always use the rate that applies to your product, service and location, and confirm it with your local tax authority or accountant, since rates and rules change.

Adding a Service Charge and Tax

If you add a service charge as well as tax, work in two steps: first add the service charge to the base price, then add tax using the calculator. Whether tax applies to the service charge depends on local rules, so check them for your case.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I remove GST from a price?

Divide the total by (1 + rate ÷ 100). The difference between the total and that result is the tax. For example, 118 at 18% GST gives a net price of 100 and tax of 18.

How do I add VAT to a price?

Multiply the net price by (1 + rate ÷ 100). For example, 100 with 15% VAT becomes 115.

What is the difference between GST and VAT?

Both are consumption taxes charged on goods and services. The name and rates depend on the country. See our GST/VAT calculation guide for worked examples.

How do I remove 18% GST from a total?

Divide the tax-inclusive total by 1.18. For example, 11,800 divided by 1.18 gives a net price of 10,000, so the GST is 1,800. Do not multiply the total by 18%, because that gives 2,124, which is wrong.

How do I calculate VAT backwards from a gross price?

Divide the gross price by (1 + VAT rate). At 20% VAT, a gross price of 120 divided by 1.2 gives a net price of 100 and VAT of 20.

How do I calculate GST in Pakistan?

Choose PKR, enter the amount and the applicable sales tax rate. The standard federal sales tax rate has commonly been 18%, but rates differ by goods, services and province and can change, so confirm the current rate with the FBR or your accountant.

Is GST the same as sales tax?

They are the same idea: a consumption tax added to the price of goods and services. The name (GST, VAT or sales tax) and the rate depend on the country.

Can I put the tax on an invoice?

Yes. Use the invoice generator and enter the tax rate to produce a tax-ready PDF invoice.
