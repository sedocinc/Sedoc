About sedoc

We build the simple, free tools that HR teams, finance professionals, and job seekers reach for every single day.

Our Story

Why We Built sedoc

sedoc started with a simple frustration: creating a clean salary slip, offer letter, or invoice shouldn't require expensive software, a design background, or an afternoon fighting with spreadsheet formulas. Our founder saw HR executives and small business owners across Pakistan and beyond re-doing the same paperwork every month, often from scratch, in outdated templates that looked unprofessional and were easy to get wrong.

So we set out to build a single workspace where anyone — a one-person startup, a growing HR department, or a fresh graduate applying for their first job — could generate a polished, accurate document in seconds, directly in the browser, with no signup and no cost.

Our Mission

To make professional-grade HR, finance, and career documents accessible to everyone, regardless of budget, technical skill, or company size. We believe good paperwork shouldn't be a privilege reserved for teams who can afford enterprise software.
