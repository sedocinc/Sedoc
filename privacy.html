Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

Welcome to sedoc ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal data and documents are handled with the highest level of security. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our website (www.thesedoc.com) and our smart document solutions.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. We only collect the information necessary to provide our services and improve your experience.

  • Uploaded Files & Text: When you use our tools to convert, edit, or summarize files, your documents and text inputs are temporarily transmitted to our secure servers or third-party APIs for processing.
  • Usage Data: We automatically collect non-personal data such as your IP address, browser type, device type, and pages visited. This helps us monitor traffic and improve our website's performance.
  • Cookies: We use cookies to store your preferences, analyze website traffic, and serve targeted advertisements.

Zero Storage Policy: All uploaded files and generated documents are permanently and automatically deleted from our servers shortly after processing. We do not read, store, or share the contents of your documents.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • To perform the document conversions, editing, or AI summarizations you request.
  • To maintain, improve, and optimize our website's functionality.
  • To serve relevant advertisements through Google AdSense.

3. Third-Party Services & APIs

To provide high-quality services, we utilize trusted third-party service providers. These providers have their own strict privacy policies:

  • ConvertAPI: Used for processing complex file conversions (e.g., Word to PDF, Excel to PDF).
  • Mistral AI (NVIDIA): Used specifically for our AI Document Summarizer to generate intelligent text summaries. Your text is processed in real-time and is not used to train their models.

4. Google AdSense & Cookies

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our website. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites on the internet.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your browsing history.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including TLS encryption, to protect your data during transit. Many of our tools (such as the PDF Editor) process files locally within your browser using Client-Side scripts (like pdf-lib), meaning your files never even leave your computer.

6. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a child, we will take steps to delete that information immediately.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
